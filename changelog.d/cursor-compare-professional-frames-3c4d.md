## Compare derives the frame from the recorded relationship, plus a professional frame set (branch `cursor/compare-professional-frames-3c4d`) — 2026-09-14

**Trigger**: Phase 0 diagnosis of Compare's five frames found (a) work tags the add-person form already accepts (`colleague`, `coworker`, `boss`, `professor`, `mentor`) were unmapped in `SELF_OTHER_TO_COMPARE`, so a saved colleague fell through to `defaultCompareRelationType(false)` and was read through the neutral **friendship** lens, and (b) there was no working register anywhere in the interpretation layer: the frame-specific copy (`RELATION_HEADLINE`, `RELATION_ASPECT_FRAME`, `RELATION_ACTION_REGISTER`, `relationLensCaption`, `narrateHouseOverlay`, `RELATION_BODY_PRIORITY`, `RELATION_HOUSES`) had five personal entries each and nothing for work. Interpretation layer only: no aspect, orb, harmony, or score computation was touched, and `computeSynastry` still takes no relationship argument.

`[ADDED]` **Three working frames: `colleagues`, `manager-report`, `mentor-mentee`** (`packages/astro/src/compare-guidance.ts`), added to `RelationType` and to `COMPARE_RELATION_TYPES` (now eight picker frames), with a new `PROFESSIONAL_RELATION_TYPES` / `isProfessionalRelation()` classification. Every frame-specific layer got an authored working entry: headline, lens caption, aspect frame, house lens (6 work & health, 10 career & reputation, 9 beliefs & travel), body priority (`colleagues` mercury/mars/saturn, `manager-report` saturn/mercury/sun, `mentor-mentee` jupiter/saturn/mercury), house-overlay closer, and an 8-opener register pool per nature per frame (pool size matters: `pickOpener` hashes into it, and `aspect-opener-variety.test.ts` fails a pool that collapses). All tagged `FOUNDER-REVIEW: authored` and awaiting a voice pass.

`[ADDED]` **`COMPARE_RELATION_LABEL` / `compareRelationLabel()`**, an exhaustive `Record<RelationType, string>`, so both pickers and the reading header stop printing raw kebab-case identifiers ("manager-report" now reads "Manager and report"). Wired in `apps/web/app/app/compare/page.tsx` and `apps/mobile/app/(app)/compare.tsx`; the `/s` OG card got matching uppercase labels.

`[CHANGED]` **A recorded work relationship now preselects the working frame** (`suggestCompareRelationType`). `colleague`/`coworker`/`co-worker` map to `colleagues`, `boss`/`manager` to `manager-report`, `professor`/`mentor` to `mentor-mentee`. Both surfaces already applied the suggestion behind `userChoseTypeRef`, so override behavior is unchanged. Two tags with neither side `self` now infer `colleagues` when BOTH sides carry a work tag: the shared claim ("both of these people are work to me") is all the peer frame needs, and the asymmetric frames stay deliberately un-inferable, because two people tagged `boss` are not a manager and a report *of each other* and nothing in the record says which would be which. `acquaintance` stays unmapped: it carries no working claim.

`[CHANGED]` **A minor in the pairing no longer overwrites a recorded non-romantic frame with `parent-child`** (both Compare pages). The romantic clamp is untouched and still runs last and still wins; what changed is only the second branch, which used to force the age-appropriate default onto any *untouched* pairing. A saved professor or mentor of a minor is a teaching relationship, and reading it as parent-child is the same silent mis-framing this branch exists to remove. Safe by construction: `suggestCompareRelationType` can never return a romantic type (§13), the clamp above has already caught anything romantic, and `availableCompareRelationTypes(true)` still removes every romantic frame from the picker.

`[ADDED]` **Working register in `whatTheyNeed()`**: the three working frames lead with working style and decision making (real Mercury sign, one authored table per frame: `COLLEAGUE_MERCURY_HOW`, `MANAGER_MERCURY_HOW`, `MENTOR_MERCURY_HOW`, so the same Mercury sign reads differently across the three rather than being a swapped prefix, per the `FRIEND_MERCURY_HOW`/`SIBLING_MERCURY_HOW` precedent), then how the person reads respect (real Saturn sign, shared `WORK_SATURN_RESPECT`), then where friction shows under deadline (real Mars sign, shared `WORK_MARS_DEADLINE`). Respect and deadline behavior are genuinely the same reading in all three working frames, so those are one shared table each, like `MOON_NEED` is for the personal frames. Every clause is keyed to a real, engine-computed sign and omitted entirely when the sign is missing or the engine flagged it uncertain.

`[DECISION]` **The Moon (emotional need) clause is deliberately absent from the working frames.** `MOON_NEED`/`MOON_HOW` and the low-emotional-score fallback are written in the personal register ("the bond holds", "close the distance", "reassurance"), which is the wrong register for a colleague, a report, or a mentee. Which real bodies a frame surfaces is already frame-specific throughout this file (Venus is romantic-only, Saturn was parent-child-only), so this hides nothing and invents nothing.

`[FIXED]` **Two shared pair-keyed layers were leaking the personal register into the working frames.** `ASPECT_ACTION` (the body-pair tactic) and `ASPECT_SUMMARY_FRAME` (the pair summary lens) are read by every frame, and their Venus and Moon cells talk about affection, warmth, comfort, tenderness, and "the bond" — correct for partners or a parent, and disqualifying in a working reading. Caught by the new test scanning rendered professional output, not by review. Added `WORK_ASPECT_ACTION` and `WORK_ASPECT_SUMMARY_FRAME`, consulted only when `isProfessionalRelation(relType)`, each overriding exactly the 28 cells whose shared copy trips the professional register gate and nothing else: a shared cell that is already register-neutral (`mars-saturn`, `jupiter-mercury`, `mercury-saturn`, and 30 more) keeps one source of truth rather than gaining a paraphrase that can drift. Partial by half, so a pair whose `catches` is clean but whose `flows` is not overrides only `flows`. Same bodies, same aspect, same orb, same flow/friction sign: only what the body means at work changes (Venus is what someone counts as good work and how they want it credited; the Moon is morale and how a person needs to be handled).

`[TESTED]` **`packages/astro/test/professional-frames.test.ts`** (new, 20 cases) is the permanent gate: tag derivation and the un-inferable asymmetric frames; a fully authored frame (own headline, caption, label, body priority, house lens, register pool with four distinct openers per nature); working-style, respect, and deadline copy for every sign, and clause omission when a sign is missing; the reading leads with the working clause and never the emotional one; and an **exhaustive** register scan of all 55 body pairs of the ten `BodyName`s in both harmony directions through both shared pair-keyed layers, against a forbidden-term list covering romantic, attraction, and intimacy language plus the personal-register affect vocabulary. That scan also asserts a non-empty pair tactic for all 55, which proves the working frames never reach the single-body `BODY_FLOW_ACTION`/`BODY_FRICTION_ACTION` fallback (whose `venus` and `mercury` lines would themselves trip the gate). "Feeling", "mood", "care", and "hurt" are deliberately NOT banned: a working reading may honestly say a mood shifted.

`[TESTED]` **`apps/web/lib/compare-professional-frames-wiring.test.ts`** (new) covers the cross-surface half: both pickers render through `compareRelationLabel`, the OG card labels all eight frames, the page's two-effect preselection order resolves each work tag to the right frame (and a user override still wins), a professor of a minor keeps `mentor-mentee` while an untouched pairing with nothing recorded still gets `parent-child` and a `partner` suggestion is still clamped, and the Vela handoff cannot romanticise a working frame.

`[DECISION]` **The working frames are intentionally NOT added to Vela's minor allowlist** (`SAFE_VELA_RELATIONSHIP_TYPES_WITH_MINOR`). With a minor in scope they coerce to `general`, which is the safe outcome (no romantic framing, and no personal frame applied to a working relationship either) and keeps `packages/vela` byte-identical to its `supabase/functions/vela-chat` mirror, so `safe-relationship-types-parity.test.ts` and the edge deploy are untouched. Asserted explicitly rather than left to chance.
