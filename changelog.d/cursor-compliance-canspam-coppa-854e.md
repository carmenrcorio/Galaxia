## CAN-SPAM footer, privacy subprocessors, COPPA age gate (branch `cursor/compliance-canspam-coppa-854e`) — 2026-09-12

**Trigger**: Three open compliance gaps flagged for CAN-SPAM and COPPA: the Privacy Policy's subprocessor table was missing three real third-party processors (Sentry, Open-Meteo, Expo) and still had the open `[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`/mailing-address placeholder in Section 15; every marketing email (trial emails, the daily nudge email) needed a real physical address and a working opt-out, not the long-standing `[MAILING ADDRESS]` placeholder; and account creation had no age confirmation on either client. No business logic, astrology calculation, or database schema changed.

`[ADDED]` **`content/legal/privacy-policy.md` Section 7 subprocessor table** gained a `Location` column and three rows: Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) / error monitoring / United States, Open-Meteo / weather and astronomical data / Switzerland, Expo (Expo Technology, Inc.) / mobile push notifications / United States. The six existing rows (Supabase, Vercel, RevenueCat, Stripe, Resend, Anthropic) got a `United States` location value so the table stays a valid single markdown table.

`[FIXED]` **Section 15 Contact us** — replaced the open `[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]` / `[MAILING ADDRESS, if you choose or are required to provide one]` placeholders with the real legal entity and address: **GALAXIA MEA LLC**, 1 Shadowrock Ct, Simpsonville, SC 29680.

`[FIXED]` **`apps/web/lib/emails.ts`** gained a shared `complianceFooterHtml`/`complianceFooterText` pair (legal entity + real mailing address + "why you're receiving this" line + a working unsubscribe link) and every marketing email now renders it: the five trial emails (`day1Email`, `day4MultiEmail`, `day4OneEmail`, `day11Email`, `day14Email`) and the daily nudge email (`skyTodayEmail`). This retires the long-open `[MAILING ADDRESS]` placeholder (previously a documented CAN-SPAM ship blocker in `changelog.d/cursor-nudge-email-delivery-9c94.md` and the doc comment on `apps/web/app/api/cron/nudge-send/route.ts`, updated here to reflect the resolution). Password reset / magic-link emails are untouched — those are sent by Supabase Auth's own GoTrue templates, not through this module, so there was nothing to add a footer to.

`[DECISION]` **Trial-email unsubscribe link points at `/account/notifications`, not a token URL**, even though `profiles.unsubscribe_token` already exists (it governs only `daily_nudge_emails_enabled`, an unrelated flag). Trial emails have no per-category consent column of their own, and adding one would be a schema change out of scope for this PR, so their opt-out points at the real, existing notification-preference surface instead of a token that would silently flip the wrong setting. `skyTodayEmail` keeps its existing, already-wired `profiles.unsubscribe_token` + `/api/nudge-email/unsubscribe` mechanism unchanged, just with updated visible footer copy.

`[ADDED]` **`apps/web/app/account/notifications/page.tsx`** — a thin redirect to `/app/settings` (the "Daily sky email" toggle, the one real email-preference control that exists today), reachable at the URL referenced by the new trial-email footer. Already behind the existing `/account/:path*` auth gate in `middleware.ts`; no new gating logic added.

`[ADDED]` **COPPA age gate on account creation only.** `apps/web/components/signup-form.tsx` gained a required `data-testid="age-gate-checkbox"` checkbox ("I confirm I am 18 years of age or older.") above the "Create account" button; the button stays disabled until it is checked. `apps/mobile/app/index.tsx`'s combined sign-in/sign-up screen gained the same label via a `TouchableOpacity` + drawn box checkbox (matching the token-driven styling the rest of the screen already uses, since `@galaxia/ui` has no existing checkbox primitive), gating only the "Create account" press, never "Sign in". Purely client-side in both places: no birth-data column, no server-side enforcement, no schema change.
