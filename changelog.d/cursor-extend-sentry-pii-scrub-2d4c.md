## Extend Sentry PII scrub to all identifier routes (branch `cursor/extend-sentry-pii-scrub-2d4c`) — 2026-09-14

**Trigger**: The Sentry scrub dropped bodies and query strings on `/api/quick-chart`, `/s/`, and `/app/`, but left person ids, invite/share tokens, and name-bearing slugs in the URL, breadcrumbs, tags, and context. `/invite/[token]`, `/r/[slug]`, and `/admin/users/[id]` were outside the prefix list entirely.

`[FIXED]` **`apps/web/lib/monitoring/scrub.ts` redacts identifier values by route pattern.** Collection nouns (`person`, `users`, `threads`, `invite`, `posts`, `support`) and single-letter prefixes (`/s/`, `/r/`) have their next path segment replaced with `[redacted]`; UUID, hyphenless-invite, email, and mixed-case opaque-token segments are redacted anywhere they appear in a URL. The route pattern stays (`/app/person/[redacted]`, `/api/admin/users/[redacted]/comp/grant`). Public blog slugs and categories are unchanged. Privacy-bearing surfaces (Quick Chart/Compare/Share, `/s/`, `/invite/`, `/r/`, `/app/`, `/admin/`, and the matching `/api/` trees) still send no request body or query string. `beforeBreadcrumb` applies the same URL redaction as `beforeSend`.
