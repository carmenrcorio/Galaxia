## Generational layer: Malala Yousafzai's Pluto corrected to Sagittarius, Rihanna added to Scorpio (branch `cursor/fix-malala-pluto-sagittarius-02c4`) — 2026-09-13

**Trigger**: founder review of `packages/astro/src/generational-layer.ts` caught a factual error. Malala Yousafzai (born 1997-07-12) was listed as a Pluto-in-Scorpio figure. Pluto left Scorpio for good in November 1995, so a 1997 birth is Pluto in Sagittarius. A wrong placement presented as fact is exactly the failure §8 and §12 exist to prevent, and this one sat under the "Others who carried this" list where users read it as verified.

`[FIXED]` Malala Yousafzai moved from `PLUTO_SIGN_EXTENDED.Scorpio.historicalFigures` to `PLUTO_SIGN_EXTENDED.Sagittarius.historicalFigures` (appended to the existing three figures, not replacing them). Verified with the package's own ephemeris rather than by assertion: `computeGenerational("1997-07-12T12:00:00Z", "date").pluto.sign` returns `Sagittarius`. A month-by-month scan of 1995-1996 also reproduces the real ingress history including the retrograde dip (Sagittarius Feb-Apr 1995, back to Scorpio May-Nov 1995, Sagittarius from Dec 1995 onward), so the engine is not agreeing by coincidence. Both cohort-range comments (`all born 1983-1995` for Scorpio, `all born 1995-2008` for Sagittarius) already bracketed the correct years and needed no edit.

`[CHANGED]` Her `plutoBridge` copy was rewritten, not carried across. The old line ("Survived an assassination attempt for the right to learn: Scorpio Pluto as the force that refuses to be extinguished") was authored to evoke Scorpio traits, survival and intensity and the thing that cannot be destroyed, and would have read as a mismatch under a Sagittarius heading. The replacement leads on the Sagittarian register instead, outward moral conviction and widening reach, at the same length and voice as the neighboring Billie Eilish and Greta Thunberg entries. `knownFor` is carried over verbatim. Tagged `FOUNDER-REVIEW` in-file with the reason for the rewrite recorded next to it.

`[ADDED]` Rihanna (born 1988-02-20, natal Pluto in Scorpio confirmed by the same engine check) added to the Scorpio list in the vacated slot, keeping that list at five figures. Her `plutoBridge` was authored fresh for her; none of Malala's copy was reused or adapted. Tagged `FOUNDER-REVIEW`.

`[OPEN]` `HistoricalFigure` carries only `name`, `knownFor`, and `plutoBridge`. Birth years exist solely as a per-array code comment (`// FOUNDER-REVIEW: all born 1983-1995, ...`), which no test can read, so `test/generational-layer.test.ts` could not have caught this error and still cannot. Catching this class of mistake automatically requires a machine-readable `born` field per figure so a test can assert `computeGenerational(figure.born, "date").pluto.sign` equals the sign whose list the figure sits in. That is a data-shape change across all eighteen figures and every consumer of the type, so it is left as a recommendation rather than folded into this narrow correction.
