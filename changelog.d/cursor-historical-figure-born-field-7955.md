## Generational layer: `HistoricalFigure.born`, and a test that checks every figure against the Pluto sign they are filed under (branch `cursor/historical-figure-born-field-7955`) — 2026-09-13

**Trigger**: the `[OPEN]` item left by `cursor/fix-malala-pluto-sagittarius-02c4` (PR #212). `HistoricalFigure` carried only `name`, `knownFor`, and `plutoBridge`; birth years existed solely as a per-array code comment, which no test can read. A wrong Pluto sign under "Others who carried this" was therefore invisible to CI, which is how Malala Yousafzai shipped under Scorpio. §8 and §12 both demand that a placement presented as fact be checkable, so the fix is to make the date machine-readable and assert on it.

`[ADDED]` `HistoricalFigure.born`, a required string holding either a full public birth date (`"YYYY-MM-DD"`) or a bare year (`"YYYY"`) when only the year is settled. `figureBirth(born)` (exported from `packages/astro/src/generational-layer.ts`) parses it into `computeGenerational` arguments and throws on anything malformed or on a well-formed non-date like `"1997-02-31"`. A year-only value stays at `precision: "year"`, so the engine returns `confident: false` with `possibleSigns` rather than silently sampling a fabricated day: §12's first listed failure ("year-only births were silently computed for July 1 and shown as settled signs") is structurally unavailable here. Dates are sampled at 12:00 UTC so the result cannot turn on which side of midnight a birth timezone falls; the generational planets move well under a degree a day.

`[ADDED]` All 28 remaining figures backfilled with a full date rather than the birth year already in the per-array comments. Year-only is not sufficient for this file: 1972 is a Pluto boundary year (Libra from 1971-10-05, retrograde back into Virgo 1972-04-17, Libra again from 1972-07-30, all confirmed by a day-resolution scan with the package's own ephemeris that reproduces the published ingress dates), so a bare `"1972"` resolves to "Libra or Virgo" and settles nothing for Eminem. The other 27 are year-only confident, but mixing precisions to save 27 hyphens buys nothing and the full date keeps each figure's cusp margin auditable.

`[ADDED]` `packages/astro/test/generational-layer.test.ts` now recomputes every figure's natal Pluto from `born` and asserts it equals the sign key whose `historicalFigures` array the figure sits in, that the engine reports it `confident`, and that the birth year falls inside the range that list's FOUNDER-REVIEW comment claims (a test-local `COHORT_YEARS` map mirrors the comments so prose and data cannot drift apart). A cusp-margin guard additionally requires natal Pluto to sit more than 1 degree from a sign boundary, so no figure passes by a hair that the noon-UTC sampling choice or the engine's ~0.1 degree tolerance could flip. Per §8 this is not self-consistency: the engine's output is checked against the founder's hand-curated filing, and the engine's ingress dates were cross-checked against the published ephemeris. 12 tests became 77.

`[FIXED]` Biggie Smalls removed from `PLUTO_SIGN_EXTENDED.Libra.historicalFigures`, which now holds four figures. Christopher Wallace was born 1972-05-21, five weeks into that retrograde dip, so his natal Pluto is Virgo 29 degrees 24 minutes retrograde, not Libra. At 0.59 degrees from the cusp it is six times the engine's stated tolerance and astro.com agrees, so this is a real misfiling and the same defect class PR #212 corrected, found by the new test on its first run.

Moving Biggie to the Virgo list needs a `plutoBridge` written in Virgo's register: his existing line leads on "systemic imbalance", which is Libra's language of scales and fairness, and carrying it across unchanged is precisely the mistake PR #212 avoided when it rewrote Malala's bridge instead of reusing it. That is founder copy and out of scope for a data-shape change, so the reason is recorded in-file next to the gap. A follow-up should either refile him under Virgo with fresh copy or add a fifth Libra figure.
