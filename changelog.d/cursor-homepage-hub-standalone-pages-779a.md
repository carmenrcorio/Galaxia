## Rebuild homepage as a concise hub linking to standalone feature pages (branch `cursor/homepage-hub-standalone-pages-779a`) — 2026-09-09

**Trigger**: step 2 of the plan started in `cursor/split-homepage-sections-into-pages-482e` (#169) — that PR gave `#shift`/`#generations`/`#vela`/`#trust`/`#pricing` their own standalone routes (`/why-galaxia`, `/generations`, `/meet-vela`, `/security`, `/pricing`) but deliberately left the homepage's own copies of those sections in place. This PR does the follow-up: trim `/` down to a hub that previews each page and links out, instead of carrying the whole story twice.

`[REMOVED]` **`app/page.tsx`** no longer renders `<WhySection>`, `<EdgeSection>`, `<RemembranceSection>`, `<WhyNotSection>`, `<FeaturesSection>`, `<VelaExampleSection>`, `<TrustSection>`, or `<PricingSection>` in full. `<RemembranceSection>`/`<WhyNotSection>`/`<FeaturesSection>` never had their own anchor or standalone route (only `#shift`/`#generations`/`#vela`/`#trust`/`#pricing` did), so removing them without a new home would have deleted that content from the site entirely — they're now grouped into `/why-galaxia` (right after `<WhySection>`, matching their original source order) since together they tell the same "why Galaxia" story: thesis, remembrance, differentiation, mechanics.

`[ADDED]` **`components/marketing/feature-teasers.tsx`** — a 5-card grid (`.teaser-grid`/`.teaser-card` in `globals.css`), one card per standalone page, each 2-3 sentences plus a "Learn more"-style link out. Replaces the removed full sections on `/`.

`[ADDED]` **`components/marketing/hash-redirect.tsx`** — a client component mounted on the homepage. A hash fragment (`#generations`, etc.) never reaches the server, so a bookmarked `/#generations` link still requests `/`; this reads `location.hash` on mount and `router.replace`s to the matching standalone route (`#shift → /why-galaxia`, `#generations → /generations`, `#vela → /meet-vela`, `#trust → /security`, `#pricing → /pricing`).

`[CHANGED]` **`MarketingNav`** now links to the real routes (Why Galaxia, Generations, Meet Vela, Blog, Pricing) instead of in-page anchors — this also means the same nav now works correctly from any page, not just `/` (previously documented as a known limitation in `components/blog/blog-header.tsx`'s comment, since fixed there too). **`SiteFooter`** gained links to all 5 standalone pages and its old `<a href="#pricing">` became a real `Link`. **`Hero`**'s "See how it works" now points at `/why-galaxia#how`.

Verified: `pnpm --filter @galaxia/web typecheck`, `pnpm --filter @galaxia/web test` (476 tests, including `seo-crawl-foundations.test.ts`), and `pnpm --filter @galaxia/web build` all pass, with `/` and all 5 standalone routes still prerendering statically (`○`). Manually confirmed in a browser: the homepage renders the hero + 5-card teaser grid + FAQ + close + footer (not the old full sections); clicking each nav link lands on the real standalone route (checked via the URL bar); `/#generations` and `/#pricing` both redirect client-side to their new routes; `/security` and `/pricing` render correctly standalone; the mobile drawer shows all 7 expected items.
