## Planet glyph tooltips on the natal wheel (branch `cursor/wheel-planet-tooltips-3734`) — 2026-09-27

**Trigger**: the wheel is the centrepiece of the chart page, but hovering a planet glyph only boldened its ring. A reader who does not recognise ♀ had to scroll to the placement list to find out what they were looking at, and nothing on the wheel told them which row to look for.

`[ADDED]` **`planetTooltipContent` in `@galaxia/core`** (`src/planet-tooltip.ts`) is the one content model both surfaces read: planet name, sign and whole degree ("Gemini 14°"), house ordinal ("10th House"), the `Rx` badge, and the `bodyDomain` line. It stays astro-free: the caller passes the already-resolved `bodyDisplayName` / `bodyDomain` strings, so the engine is not pulled into `core`. `houseTooltipLabel` returns null outside 1 to 12 and `signDegreeLabel` shows the sign alone when a placement has no degree, so a malformed placement is left out rather than rendered as a confident fact (ENGINEERING.md §12). `placementAnchorId(body)` is the shared DOM id the wheel link and the placement row both derive, so a rename cannot orphan the link.

`[CHANGED]` **`layoutChartWheel` carries placement facts onto the glyph.** `WheelPlacement` gained optional `degree` / `house` / `retro` and `WheelPlanetGlyph` now exposes `sign` plus those three. A renderer's tooltip reads the same placement the glyph was positioned from instead of re-finding it in a second list. Geometry, angles, and every existing field are untouched.

`[ADDED]` **Web hover card** (`components/wheel-planet-tooltip.tsx`), portaled to `document.body` on the GlossaryTerm pattern so the person-page scroller cannot clip it and the `ChartImageExport` raster capture of the wheel never contains it. Anchored beside the glyph and pushed radially away from the wheel centre, clamped into the viewport, re-measured on scroll and resize. Dismiss is mouse leave plus a 300ms grace period (`PLANET_TOOLTIP_CLOSE_DELAY_MS`), cancelled by moving onto the card; Escape also closes. No scrim in hover mode, so the other glyphs stay live while a card is open. Glyphs became keyboard reachable (`role="button"`, `tabIndex`, full placement as the accessible name) only on wheels that opt in, so a plain wheel does not gain a dozen empty tab stops.

`[ADDED]` **Touch and mobile sheet.** A coarse-pointer tap on the web wheel opens the same content as a bottom sheet, because a 236px card cannot sit beside a 300px wheel on a phone without covering it. Expo gets the real thing in `src/components/wheel-planet-sheet.tsx` (glossary-tooltip glass recipe, `PanResponder` swipe-down, tap-scrim and back-gesture dismiss). Both carry a "See full reading" control.

`[ADDED]` **`ChartWheel planetTooltips` is opt-in on both platforms**, and the prop is an object with two required fields rather than a boolean: `minorSafe` so the Venus domain line can never render adult copy on a child's chart (§9), and `onSeeFullReading` so the link always has a real target (§12). Live on `/app/person/[id]`, `/chart`, and the mobile profile wheel. Deliberately not on the Compare bi-wheels (two rings of glyphs in the same space; a card would speak for the chart the reader did not point at), the share snapshot, or the PDF export. Overlay and non-interactive wheels refuse the card even when the prop is passed.

`[ADDED]` **The reading link lands on the real card.** Person page: opens the row (Sun and Moon in Big Three, everything else in Placements), switches to the Them panel, then scrolls one frame later so the target is no longer `hidden`. Quick Chart: expands the collapsed full-chart list first, since the list is closed by default. Mobile: both cards report their own offset inside the scroller on layout, so the jump lands on the card rather than a guessed height.

Tests: `packages/core/test/planet-tooltip.test.ts`, a geometry pass-through case, `apps/web/components/chart-wheel.test.tsx` (hover, sweep, 300ms grace, touch sheet, keyboard, minor-safe Venus, no-cusps, and the surfaces that must stay card-free), `apps/web/components/wheel-planet-tooltip.test.tsx` (content, side flip, viewport clamping, scroll re-measure, swipe, Escape, portal), plus web and mobile surface-wiring tests. No interpretation copy, aspect data, or wheel geometry changed. No em dashes.
