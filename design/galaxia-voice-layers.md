# Galaxia voice layers

Authoritative voice law. If a surface is not listed, infer from traffic: cold or first-impression copy is layer one; search intent and in-product vocabulary is layer two. Cite this file, not a paraphrase, when writing copy.

## LAYER ONE, the outer layer

Leads with outcome. Astrology is not the first word.

- Homepage above the fold
- App store and Play store listing (`content/store/`)
- Onboarding, the first ninety seconds
- Email subject lines
- Press materials (`/press`, this file's boilerplate)
- The for work page (`/for-work`)
- Paid social and any cold traffic (`content/ads/paid-social.md`)

Outcome, in this product: the visitor understands the people already in their life, and can show up for each bond with more intention. Relationship intelligence. Not a daily blurb. Not for strangers.

## LAYER TWO, the inner layer

Astrology language stays, precise and unapologetic, because this is where search intent already lives and where users who arrived on purpose expect the real vocabulary.

- `/chart` and `/chart/compare`
- Every blog post and both blog categories
- The wheel, placements, aspects and houses tabs
- Page metadata and structured data everywhere (titles, descriptions, Open Graph, JSON-LD)
- The how it works section of the homepage (`#how`, `<FeaturesSection>`)
- Vela's own answers, which should name the aspect it is reading

## NEVER

- Do not remove an astrology keyword from page metadata to make copy cleaner.
- Do not describe the product as an astrology app in layer one.
- Do not apologise for astrology anywhere. No scare quotes, no "if you believe in that sort of thing", no winking.
- Do not promise prediction. The sky describes how a person is built, not what will happen to them. Guidance, not fortune telling.

## Split that is easy to get wrong

The homepage is both layers on one URL. Above the fold (hero headline, lede, primary CTAs, the try-it form) is layer one. The how it works section further down is layer two and must keep natal, synastry, placements, aspects, houses. The `<title>`, meta description, Open Graph, and SoftwareApplication JSON-LD are layer two on every route, including `/`.

A store listing keyword field is search, not a subtitle. Visible subtitle and description are layer one. Hidden keywords may include astrology.

Email *subjects* are layer one. Email bodies may use inner vocabulary once the person is already a member.
