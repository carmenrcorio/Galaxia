# Batch 01: Chiron synastry (review only)

**Scope:** 55 cells: Chiron with each of 11 bodies (sun, moon, mercury, venus, mars, jupiter, saturn, uranus, neptune, pluto, north_node), five major aspects each.

**Voice:** Second-person between-you (synastry). Family-safe: parent–child, siblings, friends, partners. No predictions, no fate, no mysticism.

**Apply when approved:** `pnpm readings:apply docs/readings-review/batch-01.md` (use `--dry-run` first).

- **Short:** meaning and the sore spot share a room
- **Long:** One of you carries a big story about what should be possible; the other touches the place it still hurts to want that much. Generosity lands best when it names the limit as well as the hope.
- **Traditional basis:** Jupiter expands whatever it meets; with Chiron, textbooks split between “wounded belief” and “healer-teacher,” but both agree the contact magnifies sensitivity around growth and meaning.
